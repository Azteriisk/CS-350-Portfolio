CS 350 Module Four Assignment Guidelines and Rubric

Overview

You have been working closely with the LED you added to your circuit in Module One. This was a good beginning, but design language often needs to present more information than what a simple LED can offer. This activity will show you how to expand your capabilities and offer this functionality to future programs. 

In this lab, you will add another component to your solderless breadboard to provide an additional capability. The component you will be adding is a 16x2 display. The designation 16x2 refers to the display's capability to provide 16 characters on each of two lines.

Prompt 

Your goal is to successfully set up the tools that you will be using throughout the rest of this course. To complete this lab, you will install a display component, answer the lab questions, write a brief explanation of your process, and document any challenges you face. 

Specifically, you must work through the following steps in order: 

  1. Install components and answer the lab questions. Follow the directions in the Module Four Lab Guide linked in the Supporting Materials section.
  2. Write a brief reflection. Reflect on the process you followed and any challenges you faced. Were you able to overcome these challenges? If so, how? Are there any areas where you still need help? This is your opportunity to receive your instructor's assistance on the installation process, so be as clear in your explanation as possible.

What to Submit

Submit a Word document answering the lab questions, followed by a brief reflection on setting up your LED display and potentiometer.
