CS 350 Module Six Assignment Guidelines and Rubric

Overview

The circuit you have worked on throughout this course has grown in complexity, going from a singular LED light to a circuit with an LED display, buttons, and other LED lights. Now that the 16x2 display is installed, you can begin programming the device to show information that other circuit components are picking up.  

In this lab, you will attach a temperature and humidity sensor to the QWIIC cable you attached to your Raspberry Pi during its initial setup. You will integrate the sensor into the circuit and develop code that enables it to pass the information it collects through your Raspberry Pi and display it on the 16x2 display.

Directions 

Your goal is to successfully install the temperature and humidity sensor. To complete this lab, you must ensure your 16x2 display component meets the requirements listed in the lab guide, and you must write a brief explanation of your process while documenting any challenges you faced along the way. 

Specifically, you must address the following rubric criteria: 

  1. Install the temperature and humidity sensor. Follow the directions in the Module Six Lab Guide in the Supporting Materials section.
  2. Write a brief reflection on the process you followed and any challenges you faced. Were you able to overcome these challenges? If so, how? Are there any areas where you still need help? This is your opportunity to receive your instructor's assistance on the installation process, so be as clear in your explanation as possible. 

What to Submit

Submit your explanation of approximately 200 words as a Microsoft Word document.
